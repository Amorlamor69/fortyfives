Privacy

Forty-Fives · last updated October 16, 2026

What the game keeps

Forty-Fives has no accounts and no sign-in. Your name, settings, game in progress, standings, Night Out progress and trivia score are saved in your own browser on your own device. They are never sent to us or to anyone else. Clearing your browser's site data erases them.

What we collect

Nothing. The game has no analytics, no advertising and no tracking.

Other services

  • The site is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub may log basic request details, such as your IP address, to run and secure its service.
  • The game's fonts load from Google Fonts, so your browser contacts Google to download them.

Deleting your data

Because everything lives in your browser, you can delete it yourself: use Reset standings in the game, or clear this site's data in your browser settings.
